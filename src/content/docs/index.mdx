## Everything your PDFs need, in one app

PDFBook is a desktop application for people who keep a lot of PDFs — books, papers,
manuals, manga, scanned documents — and want one calm place to read and manage them.
Reading, library storage, and PDF tools run on your computer. Optional cloud AI sends
messages, chat context, and requested document text or page images to your chosen provider.

## Start with your first PDF

1. [Download](/download/) the installer for your platform.
2. Add a folder of PDFs as a watched path.
3. Open a book and start reading.

The free plan supports **50 books**. Pro and Lifetime remove the library cap; see the
[FAQ](/faq/) for Floating Reader limits and AI provider costs.

New here? Start with the [getting-started guide](/getting-started/).
