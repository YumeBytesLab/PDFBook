## What you can do for free

The Free library holds **up to 50 books**. The Floating Reader's first free session is unmetered; later use includes **60 minutes of active reading per calendar month**. The allowance resets on the first day of the month in UTC and is recorded on the device. Ordinary reading in the main window does not consume that Floating Reader allowance.

Pro and Lifetime remove the library cap and Floating Reader time limit. AI chat is available with your own provider; AI page-reading and app-action tools require a paid plan. Provider usage fees are separate for every plan.

## Choose and activate a license

Check the [current plans and prices](https://pdfbook.app/#pricing) rather than relying on a fixed price in this guide. In **Settings → License**, enter your license key and activate the device while online. The tab shows your status and connected devices.

Keep the key private. If a device limit is reached, deactivate an old device from the management controls and try activation again. Deactivating PDFBook is separate from cancelling a paid subscription.

## Offline use and expiry

A successfully verified paid license has a cached offline allowance of **up to 14 days**, within its validity period. Periodic internet access is still needed for verification, including Lifetime. An expired or revoked key, or a verification cache older than the allowance, can revert features to the Free limits.

## When a license is not recognized

Check the key, network connection, device list, expiry and system clock. Reconnect and verify before reinstalling. Your original PDFs remain on disk; a license problem does not require deleting them. See [troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/) or [contact support](https://pdfbook.app/contact) without posting a key publicly.
