## Can I start without paying or creating an account?

Yes. Local reading needs no account. The Free library supports **50 books**, and the Floating Reader has an unmetered first session followed by **60 active minutes per calendar month**. See [plans](/plans-and-licenses/) for paid features and license verification.

## Can I read manga or EPUB books?

Manga PDFs support right-to-left reading, single pages, spreads and vertical scrolling. EPUB and other supported e-book formats are converted to PDF with Converter before reading. CBZ/CBR archives can also be converted. DRM-protected sources are skipped.

## Does syncing a folder upload my collection?

No. A sync path re-scans a local folder and updates the catalog. It is not cloud sync and does not continuously watch the filesystem. Back up the actual PDFs as well as the app data.

## Can I highlight pages, add OCR or split PDFs?

Those tools are not included. The reader has bookmarks and book-level notes; Extractor saves embedded images. It does not split a page range into another PDF. Creator builds PDFs from JPG/PNG images.

## Is AI included, and does it see all my books?

AI is optional and uses your provider or local Ollama model. Provider fees are separate. Chat is available for Free; page-reading and app-action tools need a paid plan. Opening a book does not automatically upload its entire contents. Requested text, page images and context can be sent to a configured cloud provider.

## Why are some controls unavailable on my computer?

Screen capture works on Windows and Linux X11; it is disabled on macOS and Wayland. Automatic turning also depends on the native helper and CPU. Store distribution is being prepared; use a regular build from [Downloads](/download/) for now. More answers are in [troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/).
