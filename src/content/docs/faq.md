## Is PDFBook free?

The free plan supports a library of **50 books**. The Floating Reader has an unmetered first session, then **60 minutes of active reading per calendar month**. Pro and Lifetime remove those two limits. [Current plans and pricing](https://pdfbook.app/#pricing) are on the website.

## Which computers are supported?

Windows 10 / 11, macOS, and Linux, with x64 and ARM64 downloads. Some features have platform limits: screen capture is supported on Windows and Linux X11, not on macOS or Linux Wayland. See [Download](/download/) and [Screen capture](/features/screen-capture/).

## Does PDFBook upload my PDFs?

Reading, library storage, PDF creation, and conversion run locally. Reading requires no account. Optional cloud AI sends messages, chat context, and any requested document text or page images to your chosen provider. A local Ollama model keeps those AI requests on your machine. See the [privacy policy](https://pdfbook.app/privacy).

## Can I read manga and password-protected PDFs?

Yes. The [reader](/features/reader/) supports right-to-left reading, spreads, and vertical scrolling. Enter a document's password when asked; you can choose to remember it for that book.

## Can I annotate PDFs or extract pages into another PDF?

PDFBook has bookmarks and notes attached to a book, but not highlighting, drawing, or page annotations. The Extractor saves **embedded images**, not a range of pages as a new PDF. See [PDF tools](/features/creating-pdfs/).

## Is an AI subscription included?

No. Connect your own provider or local model. Free users can chat; page-reading and app-action tools require a paid PDFBook plan. Provider fees are separate. [AI guide](/features/ai-assistant/).

## How do I get updates or support?

Standalone builds can check for releases on startup when enabled in Settings. Download the matching build from [Releases](https://github.com/YumeBytesLab/PDFBook/releases/latest). Microsoft Store distribution is still being prepared.

[Report a bug](https://github.com/YumeBytesLab/PDFBook/issues/new/choose), [ask a question](https://github.com/YumeBytesLab/PDFBook/discussions), or [contact support](https://pdfbook.app/contact). Include the version, OS, CPU, and reproduction steps; keep private documents out of public reports.
